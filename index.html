About Me
UX/UI Designer

I design with purpose

I'm a UX Designer focused on creating intuitive, user-centered digital products for web and mobile. I bring together UX research, interaction design, and design systems to turn ideas into clear, functional interfaces that actually solve problems.

Based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, I've worked across a range of industries — from healthcare apps and civic tech to SaaS platforms and fashion e-commerce. Every project starts with understanding real user pain points, not just aesthetics.

Confident in Figma, prototyping, and cross-functional collaboration, I bring designs from concept to handoff with systems-thinking at every step. Currently available for freelance projects worldwide.
